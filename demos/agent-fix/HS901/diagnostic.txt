HS901: 'BookingConfirmed' is both an entity and a domain event: it implements 'IEntity' and 'IDomainEvent'. A type is either an entity, which has an identity and changes through its methods, or a domain event, an immutable fact an entity records. Fix: keep 'BookingConfirmed' as the entity and declare the event as a separate record that it records. Skill: fix-architecture-violation. Doc: 'docs/rules/HS901.md'.
