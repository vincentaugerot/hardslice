## Path

**1. Skills.** Before any command, the agent loaded `add-module`, `add-aggregate`, `add-slice` and `add-domain-event`, in that order: the request names none of them, `AGENTS.md` and their descriptions led to them.

**2. Templates.** Each element came from its template, in this order, each followed by a read of what it generated:

```text
dotnet new hardslice-module --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --name Catalog
dotnet new hardslice-aggregate --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --module Catalog --name Product
dotnet new hardslice-aggregate --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --module Catalog --name Stock --table Stocks
dotnet new hardslice-event --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --module Catalog --name ProductRegistered --reaction Catalog/OpenStockOnProductRegistered
dotnet new hardslice-command --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --module Catalog --name RegisterProduct
dotnet new hardslice-query --project src/Shop/Shop.csproj --module Catalog --name GetProduct --aggregate Product
dotnet ef migrations add AddCatalog --project src/Shop --output-dir Shared/Persistence/Migrations
```

It added the lines the module template printed to `Program.cs`, the registrations of both slices and of the reaction to `CatalogModule.cs`, and the module to the map of `AGENTS.md`: `Catalog`, its mappings, its command, its query and its reaction.

**3. Domain.** The rules of the request are in the aggregates, never in a handler:

- `Product.Register(name, price)` throws a `DomainException` for a missing name, a name over 100 characters and a price that is not greater than zero, and records `ProductRegistered(ProductId)`;
- `Stock.Open(productId)` opens a stock at zero, holding the `ProductId` only (HS008). `Stock` has no other method: the agent pointed out that a quantity which never changes stays non-negative, and that the request asked for no change of quantity.

Unit tests: 8 for `Product`, one per rule, the boundary of 100 characters and the event; 3 for `Stock`.

**4. Slices.** `RegisterProduct` checks the shape of its input in its validator (required name, at most `Product.NameMaxLength`, price greater than zero), calls `Product.Register`, adds the product and returns its `Guid`, without `SaveChangesAsync`. `GetProduct` projects `Id`, `Name` and `Price` without tracking, and answers 404 for an unknown identifier. `OpenStockOnProductRegistered` adds `Stock.Open(domainEvent.ProductId)` through `AppDbContext`, inside the transaction of the command. The migration maps `Products` and `Stocks`, with a unique index on `Stocks.ProductId` that the agent added and pointed out. Tests: 5 of the validator; integration tests of the registration (stock opened at zero read back from PostgreSQL, 400 of an invalid request), of the read (the product, the 404) and of the reaction.
